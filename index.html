Transparent & Fair

How TRKM Rankings Work

TRKM rankings are based on six main areas. Each area has a fixed weight for clear, balanced evaluation.

20%

Academic Excellence

Teaching quality, faculty strength, and comprehensive course content that prepares students for success.

20%

Research & Innovation

Research output, innovation metrics, and industry collaboration projects that drive progress.

20%

Employability

Job placement rates, graduate salary levels, and employer satisfaction with university alumni.

15%

Global Engagement

International students, diverse faculty, and global partnerships that broaden perspectives.

15%

Social Impact

Community engagement, sustainability initiatives, and contributions to solving global challenges.

10%

Digital Readiness

Technology adoption, online learning capabilities, and preparation for digital future skills.

Data & Transparency

TRKM collects data from multiple trusted sources and follows clear, fair practices:

  • Universities: Direct data submission and verification
  • Employers: Graduate hiring surveys and feedback
  • Public Data: Government and institutional databases
  • Independent Research: Third-party validation and analysis
